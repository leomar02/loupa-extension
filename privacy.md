title: Loupa — Privacy Policy
---


_Last updated: 2026-08-11_

Loupa is a Chrome extension that adds badges (posting date, salary, workplace
type, promoted/Easy Apply flags) and a sort/filter toolbar to job listings on
LinkedIn.

## Data collection

**Loupa does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.**

- It reads the job-listing text that is already visible on the LinkedIn pages
  you open, only to display badges and to sort/filter those cards.
- All processing happens **locally in your browser**. Nothing is ever sent to any
  server, and the extension makes no network requests of its own.
- No analytics, no tracking, no cookies, no accounts.
- The extension requests no browser permissions beyond running its content script
  on LinkedIn job pages.

## What it accesses

Loupa's content script runs only on:

- `https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/*`

On those pages it reads the visible listing content to render badges and reorder
cards. It does not access your account, messages, browsing history, or any other
site.

(The codebase also contains support for Indeed, but it is not enabled in the
published extension and no Indeed permission is requested.)

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted in the extension's
repository.

## Contact

Questions: open an issue on the project repository, or contact the developer at
the email listed on the Chrome Web Store listing.
